User guide

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Add books

Open 'Add book files' under My. Choose a file with 'Add a file'. EPUB, PDF, and TXT formats are detected automatically.

For several books, tap 'Add books from a folder'. Choose the books to add from the folder. Added files are copied into the app.

Add more files to the same work. Tap '+ Add file' in the 'File' row on the work info screen.

Tap the cover on the work info screen. Tap 'Choose from file covers' or 'Choose from gallery'. You can also attach a file with '+ Add file'.

Reading and appearance

Open a book and tap the center of the screen. The menu opens; tap again to close it.

The default page-turn direction is 'Left and right'. Tap the left edge for the previous page. Tap the right edge for the next page.

Change 'Page-turn tap zones' in 'Viewer settings'. With 'Top and bottom', the top opens the previous page. The bottom opens the next page.

For EPUB and TXT, choose a font in 'Appearance'. Also adjust text size, spacing, and background. Text size stays fixed in original PDFs.

With 'Scroll view', swipe to read. Tapping the edges does not turn pages in this mode.

Highlights, memos, and bookmarks

Press and hold a sentence in an EPUB or TXT book. Adjust the selection and tap the color button. The selected passage is saved as a highlight.

Tap a saved highlight, then its color button. Choose yellow, green, purple, blue, red, or gray.

Select a passage and tap 'Memo'. Write your note and tap 'Save'.

Use the bookmark button in the menu to save your position. Tap it again at the same position to remove it. Review saved records in 'Reading notes'.

Convert an original PDF before highlighting it. Choose 'Convert to EPUB' from the menu. Image scans without text cannot be converted.

Write notes (episode 10, volume 17, page 203)

Write in the work’s note input box. Start with '10화', '17권', or '203쪽' for episode 10, volume 17, or page 203. Follow the marker with a space or a line break.

You can also write !10 for episode 10. Use @17 for volume 17 or p203 for page 203. Write your note after the marker.

For several episodes, use 10-12화 or !10-12. Follow the marker with a space or a line break.

Without a marker, the text becomes an overall note. You can also leave a single verdict on the whole book. Use 'Overall review' at the top.

Organize your library and shelves

Open 'Shelf list' in Library. Create a shelf with '+ Add new shelf'. Press and hold a work and choose 'Add to shelf'.

Use 'Change reading status' to change a work’s status. Works with book files appear automatically on the matching status shelves.

The 'Reading' shelf also includes serializing works and rereads. The 'Completed' shelf also includes completed serials.

There are also 'Not started', 'Dropped', and 'Records only' shelves. These three are hidden at first. Choose which shelves to show using 'Edit' in 'Shelf list'.

'Records only' holds works without book files. 'Remove from shelf' keeps the work and its records.

Calendar and statistics

Reading in the viewer or saving a note records a reading day. Tap a date in 'Calendar' to see its works.

Fill in missing days manually. Tap a date and use 'Add book'. See a year’s reading days in 'Year'.

Check 'Monthly summary' in 'Stats'. 'Books read' and 'Notes' are based on saved notes. 'Reading days' counts the days you read.

See your current and longest streaks in 'Reading streak'. Also check platform and type shares and the rating distribution.

Backup

Open 'Backup' under My. Use 'Create backup file' to save your records and settings.

Backups use a password by default. Set a password with at least eight characters. A forgotten password cannot be recovered to open the backup.

'Include original book files' is on at first. Turn it off to leave book files out of the backup. You will need to add the book files again after restoring.

Choose a file with 'Open backup file'. Review its contents and tap 'Restore this backup'. The backup replaces your current records and settings.

On e-ink devices

E-ink means an electronic ink display. These settings also apply to color e-ink devices such as Onyx readers.

Turn on 'App-wide high contrast (E Ink)' under My. It increases text and line contrast in the light theme. It does not apply in a dark theme.

Tap the center of the reading screen. Turn on 'E Ink mode' in 'Viewer settings'. The background becomes white and the text black.

E-ink mode reduces screen animations. Page-turn effects are also disabled. Set it separately from app-wide high contrast.

For lingering images, turn on 'Periodic full refresh'. Set the interval with 'Full refresh interval'. The whole screen redraws after the chosen number of pages.
